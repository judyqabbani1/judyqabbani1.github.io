The game

You play Mr. Bladder, a container-shaped guy living in an organ-themed world. Finish your everyday tasks, explore the town and talk to NPCs, all while holding it in so you don’t spill in public. Movement and balance are the core of the game, which is why the camera and controls matter so much.

What I’m building

  • In progress
    Right-stick camera rotation with camera-relative movement

    Letting the player orbit the camera with the controller’s right stick, and making character movement follow the camera’s direction so pushing forward always means “away from the camera.”

  • Done
    Toilet audio bug fix and Wwise integration

    Tracked down a bug in the toilet sound effect, fixed it, and brought the sound into the game through Wwise.

Working on the team

AGP is a large, cross-disciplinary team: engineers, 3D artists and animators, VFX and tech art, audio, usability research, production and QA. I work in Unreal with C++ and Blueprints and use Perforce and Git for version control.
