About

I came to games through software engineering. I built web features in Angular and Node.js, automated workflows with UiPath, and spent a year at IBM presenting technical solutions to clients. That taught me how to explain technical ideas to people who aren’t engineers, which matters a lot on a game team.

Games were always the goal. At Alfaisal University I was president of The Gamers Club, and now I’m at USC studying 3D graphics and interactive design and writing gameplay code on two team projects every week.

What I care about most is how a game feels in your hands: the camera, the controls, and the small bits of feedback that make them work.
